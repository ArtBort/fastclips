fast clipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

fast clipper is a personal desktop tool made by Bort_ (Bart Lefèvre) that posts gameplay clips to Bort_'s own TikTok account.

What data is used

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected.
  • A TikTok access token, stored only on the owner's own computer, to upload and post videos.
  • The video clips and captions the owner chooses to post.

What is not done

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data.
